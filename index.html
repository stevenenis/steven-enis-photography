About

Whatever catches the eye earns a frame.

Steven Enis is a photographer working across portraits, fashion, and commercial spaces. He shoots headshots that look like the person on their best ordinary day, models and lookbooks with a natural edge, and interiors and brand work that need to feel like somewhere real — on location, worldwide.

The instinct behind it was built on the street: reading light on the move, and catching the moment that only happens once. That same eye goes into every commission — unforced, quick, and honest to whoever, or whatever, is in front of the lens.

How I work

Direction

At ease

Quick to read a room and easy to shoot with, so people loosen up and the frame looks like them — not a pose.

Light

As found

Works with the light that's there and shapes it when a shoot needs it — flattering for faces, honest for spaces.

Timing

The moment

A street-trained sense for the frame that only happens once, brought to every brief and deadline.
